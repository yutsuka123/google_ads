About Nyangailab

We build small, delightful Android apps. Our priorities are clarity, low battery usage, and keeping personal data on the device whenever possible.

Nyangailab is an independent developer in Tokyo. Besides the apps, we make web tools for learning: Science Lab for physics and an English learning app. See the About page for how we work and how to reach us, and the Privacy page for how data is handled.
